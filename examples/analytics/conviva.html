Conviva analytics

Example of basic DASH playback with Conviva analytics enabled.

Enter the customer key from your Conviva account, then load the stream.

Setup required: This page sends no data to Conviva until you add the Conviva Core SDK (4.0.3 or later). Conviva provides the SDK directly to its customers; it isn't available on npm. Replace TODO/path/to/conviva-core-sdk.js in examples/analytics/conviva.html with the path to your copy.

Test gateway for development. Leave empty in production.
