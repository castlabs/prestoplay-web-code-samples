Broadpeak analytics and SSAI

Example of DASH playback with Broadpeak analytics and tracking of server-side inserted (SSAI) ads, with an ad countdown, a skip button, and an ad event log.

Setup required: Broadpeak sessions only start for streams delivered by a Broadpeak CDN. Enter a stream URL from your Broadpeak CDN, its domain, and your analytics server, then load the stream. The default castLabs stream plays without a Broadpeak session.
