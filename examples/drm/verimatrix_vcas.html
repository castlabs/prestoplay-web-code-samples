Example of DRM-protected DASH or HLS playback with licenses from Verimatrix VCAS Ultra.

Setup required: Enter a stream protected with Verimatrix VCAS Ultra, the device ID, and your license servers, then load the stream. Only the license servers for the key systems you use are needed: Widevine on Chrome, Firefox, and Edge, PlayReady on Edge and smart TVs, and FairPlay on Safari.
